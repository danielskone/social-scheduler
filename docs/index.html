Viral Post is a private scheduling tool that Daniel King built for his own use. It schedules and publishes videos and posts to his own Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts, including the Fuquay-Varina Christmas Parade channels. It runs only on his own computer and has no other users.
