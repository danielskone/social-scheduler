Viral Post is a social media scheduler for creators, small businesses and community groups. Plan your posts on one calendar, schedule them for the best time, and publish your own photos and videos to Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. Then see how they did, all in one place.

Made by Daniel King in Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina. Start a free trial.

What you can do

  • Write a post once and schedule it to several of your accounts, each at its own time.
  • See every upcoming post on a calendar, and move or change it before it goes out.
  • Turn a long video you made into short clips with captions.
  • See views, likes and comments for your posts.

Works with

YouTube (uses YouTube API Services)

Uploading to YouTube uses the YouTube API Services. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok. Viral Post signs in with TikTok's official Login Kit and posts with the TikTok Content Posting API. Before each TikTok post you see which account it goes to, choose who can see it, choose whether people can comment, duet or stitch, and say whether it promotes a brand. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you press Schedule or Post now.

Facebook and Instagram. Viral Post uses Meta's official Graph API to post to the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts you connect.
