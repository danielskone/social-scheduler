Viral Post is a social media scheduler run by Daniel King. This policy explains what Viral Post stores when you use it, why, and how to remove it.

What information is collected

  • Your Viral Post account: your email address and a protected (hashed) password. If you pay for a plan, payments are handled by Stripe; Viral Post never sees or stores your card number.
  • From TikTok: your TikTok account's ID, display name, username and profile picture; a sign-in token that lets Viral Post post the videos you schedule; the IDs of videos posted through Viral Post and of your public videos (to match them with your posts); and statistics for those videos (views, likes, comments, shares) and your follower count.
  • From YouTube / Google: the connected channel's ID, name and picture; a sign-in token that lets the app upload to that channel; the IDs of videos it uploaded; and public statistics for those videos (views, likes, comments) and the channel's subscriber count.
  • From Facebook and Instagram: the Pages and Instagram accounts you connect (ID, name, picture), sign-in tokens, the IDs of posts made through Viral Post, and their statistics and comments.
  • Content you create: the captions, titles, photos and videos you schedule, and your choices for each post.

How it is used

Only to publish the posts and videos you schedule, at the time you choose, with the settings you choose, and to show you how your own posts are performing. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling, and nothing is posted without your action.

YouTube API Services

Viral Post uses YouTube API Services to upload your videos to your YouTube channel and to show statistics for those videos. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Where it is stored and who it is shared with

Your information is stored on the computer that runs Viral Post, operated by Daniel King, in a separate space for each Viral Post account. It is not sold or shared with anyone. It is sent only to the platform you choose to post to (for example, your video is sent to TikTok when you post to TikTok), and to Stripe for payments.

Cookies

Viral Post sets one cookie to keep you signed in. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • Statistics are refreshed regularly. YouTube data that has not been refreshed within 30 days is deleted or refreshed.
  • Click Remove next to an account on the Accounts page to delete its sign-in token, its statistics and its post history from Viral Post straight away.
  • You can also revoke Viral Post's access at any time: for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions; for Google at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; for Facebook and Instagram at facebook.com/settings, Business Integrations.
  • To delete your whole Viral Post account and everything in it, or to ask any question about your data, use the contact below. It will be deleted within 7 days.
