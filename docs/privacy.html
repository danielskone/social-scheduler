Viral Post is a private tool used only by its owner, Daniel King. It runs only on his own computer. This policy explains what it stores and how to remove it.

YouTube API Services

Viral Post uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to his own YouTube channel and to show statistics for those videos. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What information is collected

  • From YouTube / Google: the connected channel's ID, name and picture; a sign-in token that lets the app upload to that channel; the IDs of videos it uploaded; and public statistics for those videos (views, likes, comments) and the channel's subscriber count.
  • From other platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok): the same kinds of account information, sign-in tokens and post statistics for the owner's own accounts.
  • Content you create: the captions, titles, photos and videos you schedule.

How it is used

Only to publish the posts and videos you schedule, at the time you choose, and to show you how your own posts are performing. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored and who it is shared with

Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer. Viral Post has no servers of its own. Information is not sold or shared with anyone. It is sent only to the platform you choose to post to (for example, your video is sent to YouTube when you post to YouTube).

Cookies

Viral Post sets one cookie, on your own devices only, to keep you signed in to the app. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • Statistics from YouTube are refreshed regularly. YouTube data that has not been refreshed within 30 days is deleted or refreshed.
  • Click Remove next to an account on the Accounts page to delete its sign-in token, its statistics and its post history from the app straight away.
  • You can also revoke Viral Post's access to your Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After that, the app can no longer access your YouTube channel.
  • To ask for any data to be deleted, use the contact below. It will be deleted within 7 days.
