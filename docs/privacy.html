Privacy Policy

Viral Post runs only on its owner's own computer. It stores, on that computer only: the sign-in keys for the owner's connected accounts, the posts and videos he schedules, and basic statistics (views, likes, comments, followers) for his own posts.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is sent only to the platform the owner chooses to post to (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram). Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its keys.
